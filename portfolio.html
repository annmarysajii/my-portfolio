One person.

I am Annmary Saji, a BFA Animation graduate from NTU's School of Art, Design and Media, class of June 2026. Over five years I built a creative practice that refuses to stay in one lane. I animate, illustrate, shoot video, design brand systems, and produce music — often all within a single project.

My work has screened at Annecy as part of the Paris Lyon Singapore programme, reached audiences of 33,000 on campus, and shipped for clients from international fintech firms to indie startups. I am multilingual, internationally minded, and drawn to what happens when disciplines stop being separate categories and start talking to each other.
