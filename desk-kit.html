Desk Kit

You are looking down at someone's desk. The work lies flat. The person is in the drawers. Everything on the site is cut from the same five parts.

Narrative

Four metaphors, one job each

The site currently runs a book, a drawer and a desk at the same time, with no rule about which means what — so it reads as a lot of nice paper rather than one place. The fix isn't fewer metaphors. It's giving each one a job.

Desk The place Always underneath, never interactive. It is simply where you are. SRF-01
Sheets The work Professional output. Laid out flat, nothing hidden, scrolled down through. SRF-02 · CNT-*
Drawers The person Anything personal is behind something you pull open. All seven reveals are drawers. EDG-02
Stamps Your trace What the visitor leaves behind. Opening a drawer is what earns one. MRK-01

The rule that makes it hold: work lies flat, the person is in a drawer. It decides where anything new belongs, and it turns seven scattered easter eggs into one repeated gesture.

Diagnosis

The metaphor doesn't fade because you ran out of ideas

9 separate implementations of tape. Zero shared components. .hero-tape-top · .hero-tape-bottom · .about-tape-tl · .about-tape-tr · .notebook-tape-tl ×2 · .polaroid-tape · .notes-pin-tape ×2 · .scrap-tape-corner
1 mention of the cutting mat in the entire file — and it's inside a code comment. The surface that the whole concept rests on exists only in the hero, and only by implication.
0 surface treatment on .sec, which carries scroll-margin-top and nothing else. The five middle sections are flat colour fields. Nothing about them is a physical object.

Each idea was built once, in place, and never became a part. Cohesion isn't a new concept — it's the same nine tapes, defined once.

Build order

Three passes, in this order

The order matters: the first pass makes the other two cheap. Doing it backwards means building the same nine tapes a tenth time.

Pass 01 · foundation

Make the parts exist

Nothing visibly new. This is the pass that buys the cohesion.

  • Collapse the nine tape classes into one component with variants, driven by custom properties for colour and angle.
  • Put the mat behind the whole page, not just the hero.
  • Give .sec a real sheet — paper, lift, and an overlap onto the section above.
Pass 02 · identity

Give each section its object

Now that the parts exist, each section is an assembly, not a bespoke build.

  • Peg bar, sketchbook, contact sheet, pinned proofs, J-card — one per section.
  • Index tabs return the numerals you removed, as a physical thing rather than a label.
  • Work cards inherit the container their section uses.
Pass 03 · life

Make it move and reward

The motion pass already written — it lands better once the objects are real.

  • Logo placement, badge stamping, doodle drift, the one-time nudge.
  • Passport stamps become MRK-01 marks on the page, not a separate counter.
  • Lazy-load everything below the first sheet.
Parts are referenced by code throughout — SRF surface, FST fastener, CNT container, MRK marker, EDG edge. Every specimen on this page is pure CSS, no images, and is meant to be lifted directly.
