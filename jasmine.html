Overview

What is Jasmine?

Jasmine is a multimedia world built around the concept of an Indo-jazz fusion bar called "Jazzmine" — a space where Indian classical music traditions meet the improvisational language of jazz. The project began as a music EP and grew into a full-spectrum creative world: character designs, environment concept art, comic panels, and a planned animated short, all orbiting the same fictional universe.

"A place where every note remembers where it came from."

World-Building

The Jasmine Universe

Every element of the Jasmine world is designed to be self-consistent — from the architecture of the bar to the instruments the characters play. Each discipline feeds the others: the music informs the character moods, the character designs influence the comic layouts, and the comic panels map scenes for the eventual animation.

Process & Reflection

How the disciplines talk to each other

Jasmine started as a music question: what would a jazz fusion EP sound like if it was rooted in Indian classical music rather than American blues? Once the first two tracks existed, I realised I couldn't hear the world without seeing it. The characters arrived next — regulars of the bar, each tied to a different instrument and a different narrative thread. The comic panels came from the need to show time passing between tracks. The animation is where all of it will finally move.

The project is the fullest answer I have to why I don't want to pick one medium. Each discipline here is doing something the others cannot.
