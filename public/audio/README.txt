Room sound for the hero and the desk (opt-in: the visitor turns it on with the speaker button in the nav).
Nothing is synthesised. The system tries these extensions in order and uses the first that exists: .mp3, .ogg.

MUSIC (loops, loudness about -18 LUFS, last 3s crossfaded into the first 3s so the loop has no click)
  day-music     Jazz Rainy Lounge, Alex Morgan
  night-music   2 AM Lofi Chill Ambient, Music For Videos

EFFECTS (short, mono, peak about -3 dBFS; levels are the --snd-* variables in the page CSS)
  paper-1/2/3   a sheet moving; three pitches of one recording so it never repeats exactly. Played, quietly, when a
                mouse touches a sheet, print, card or the notebook (at most once every 1.1s).
  drawer-open   the drawer sliding out (quick-look drawer, and the Studio Desk drawer under the About badge)
  drawer-close  the same recording pitched down a little: there is only one drawer recording, a real close sound is better
  lamp-click    OPTIONAL, not added yet: the lamp switch

Made from: makigai_maimai-paper-245786.mp3 and freesound_community-drawer-41055.mp3 (Downloads), trimmed to the
active part, levelled, and encoded as mp3 128k. Both look like Pixabay/Freesound files: check each licence.
