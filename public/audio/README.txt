Room sound for the hero (opt-in; the visitor turns it on with the speaker button in the nav).
Nothing here is synthesised: supply the files. The system tries these extensions in this order
and uses the first that exists: .webm (Opus), .m4a (AAC), .mp3, .ogg.

night-rain     seamless loop: quiet room tone with faint rain on glass.  (used at night)
day-birds      seamless loop: soft room tone with distant birds.         (used by day)
lamp-click     one short click, the lamp switch.
paper-rustle   one very short, soft paper sound, played very quietly when a taped item is hovered.

Keep the loops short (10 to 30 seconds), cut so the end meets the start, and compressed
(Opus/WebM is ideal). Levels are set in the page's CSS (--snd-loop, --snd-ui, --snd-rustle),
so master the files at a normal level and tune there.
